## Bikepacking food

I'm building a collection of campsite recipes and three slightly different day
menus for bikepacking. My planning target is **5,000 kcal per day**, or
**15,000 kcal for a three-day trip**.

The idea is to share enough ingredients to keep packing simple, while varying
flavours, textures and meals so I still want to eat them on day three. This starts
with overnight oats; I'll add more recipes and try the menus over time.

### Recipes

| Recipe | Dry weight per serving | Energy per serving |
|---|---:|---:|
| [Campsite overnight oats](/cycling/food/bikepacking/overnight-oats) | 320 g | About 1,261 kcal |

Food Calories are recorded as kcal. Dry weights exclude added water and packaging.
Energy estimates use the ingredient values listed with each recipe, so they can
be adjusted for different products.

### Three day menus

These are menus in progress. Each day will account for breakfast, lunch, dinner,
riding snacks and any calorie-containing drinks.

| Menu | Breakfast | Lunch, dinner and snacks | Daily target |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Day A | Overnight oats, about 1,261 kcal | About 3,739 kcal still to allocate | 5,000 kcal |
| Day B | Variation or alternative to develop | To develop | 5,000 kcal |
| Day C | Variation or alternative to develop | To develop | 5,000 kcal |

The oats are a starting point for Day A. The aim is to vary the full menus a
little, rather than repeat an identical day's food three times.

### Building the collection

For each recipe I'll keep ingredient weights, calories per serving, water and
equipment needs, preparation details, and feedback from trying it. Variations
will have their own quantities and calorie totals.

Next steps are to establish the oats preparation method, add lunches, dinners
and snacks, and develop the variations for Days B and C. Once the menus are filled
in, I'll combine their ingredients into a three-day shopping and packing list,
including total food weight.
