### Jus' Sugs
{: #jus-sugs }

Jus' Sugs is my homemade maltodextrin and fructose drink mix, using roughly a
**5:4 ratio by weight**. The original batch quantities are 555.6 g maltodextrin
and 444.4 g fructose, making **1 kg of carbohydrate powder**.

| Quantity | Maltodextrin | Fructose | Approx. energy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 serving: 60 g | 33.3 g | 26.7 g | 240 kcal |
| 2 servings: 120 g | 66.7 g | 53.3 g | 480 kcal |
| Three-day supply: 360 g | 200 g | 160 g | 1,440 kcal |
| Bulk batch: 1,000 g | 555.6 g | 444.4 g | 4,000 kcal |

Energy is estimated at 4 kcal per gram of carbohydrate. Weights refer to the
carbohydrate powders, before adding water or salt; actual product labels may
round differently. Mix a 60 g serving with drinking water, adjusting the water
quantity to taste. The [bikepacking day menus](/cycling/food/bikepacking) include
**two 60 g servings each day**.

**Jus' Sugs contains no salt.** Add salt separately as appropriate for the day's
drinking volume and environmental conditions; it is not a fixed part of this
carbohydrate recipe.
